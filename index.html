DESIGN PROTOTYPE

About the MATH 110 Study Hub

The Study Hub explores how AI could provide just-in-time support in an asynchronous statistics course while also creating opportunities for meaningful peer connection.

🤖 What the Course Assistant does

The Course Assistant provides immediate, private help when a learner gets stuck. It can explain concepts, provide examples, offer another explanation, and check understanding.

👥 Where classmates come in

After receiving AI support, learners may see relevant conversations from classmates who have wondered about similar concepts. Learners can read those perspectives without posting and choose whether they want to join the conversation.

🔒 Privacy by design

Questions asked privately to the Course Assistant are not shared with classmates. Learners explicitly choose when they want to contribute something to the course community.

What is simulated?

This is an interactive design prototype, not a fully implemented AI system. AI responses, classmate activity, and discussion posts are simulated for demonstration purposes.

A full implementation would require a course-grounded AI system, student authentication, LMS integration, persistent discussion storage, privacy controls, and moderation.
